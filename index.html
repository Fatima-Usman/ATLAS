ISLAMIYAT

Cambridge O-Level Islamiyat (2058) is structured around two written examinations, Paper 1 and Paper 2, which are each 1 hour 30 minutes long and carry equal weightage toward your final grade. Paper 1 assesses your knowledge of core Quranic passages, the complete life of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ across the Makkan and Madinan eras, and the contributions of the early Muslim community. Paper 2 focuses on selected Hadiths, the reign of the Rightly Guided Caliphs (Rāshidūn), the core Articles of Faith, and the essential Pillars of Islam. Each paper features a compulsory first question focused on passage or Hadith commentaries, a compulsory major topic question, and a choice of two out of three remaining essay questions. Securing top marks requires a balanced approach: Part (a) questions demand precise chronological detail, accurate dates, key names, and relevant Quranic or Hadith references, while Part (b) questions evaluate your ability to apply these historical teachings to the personal reflection and daily life of a modern Muslim.
